⚡ Byoroshye kandi bwihuse

Ongeramo umusaruro wawe mu masaha make, kandi abaguzi bazawubona mu buryo bwihuta.

🤝 Nta bakomiseri

Ucuruza neza ku buryo butaziguye, uhitemo uburyo bwo kuvugana kuri WhatsApp cyangwa guhamagara.

🛡️ Amakuru yizewe

Abaguzi babona ibisobanuro birambuye ku musaruro, aho uherereye, ndetse n'amafoto yizewe.

Uko bikorwa

1

✍️ Iyandikishe

2

📦 Tangira gusangiza

3

💬 Vugana n'abaguzi
